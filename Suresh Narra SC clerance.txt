This notification informs you that you have been granted security clearance, but with restrictions due to concerns related to your Indian nationality. Here’s a breakdown of what it means:

1. Security Clearance Granted with Restrictions
You do have security clearance, meaning you can work in a role that requires it.
However, there are restrictions on your access to certain caveated material (sensitive or classified information that has additional security controls).
2. Why the Restriction?
The UK government has identified concerns regarding your Indian nationality.
This does not mean you are untrustworthy, but rather that your specific circumstances might pose a security risk, such as potential foreign influence or pressure.
3. What Does This Mean for You?
Your Security Officer (SO) will explain what you can and cannot access.
You should immediately report if you ever feel at risk of pressure, blackmail, or coercion due to your nationality, activities, or personal connections.
If any personal circumstances change (e.g., change of nationality, foreign connections, financial issues), you must report it through a Change in Personal Circumstance Questionnaire.
4. Validity & Transferability
Your security clearance is valid for 10 years (12/03/2025 – 12/03/2035).
It may be transferable to other roles, but this depends on Cabinet Office Personnel Security Policy.
It can be revoked at any time if new risks arise.
5. Next Steps for You
Expect your Security Officer to contact you to clarify your restrictions.
If you have questions, you can contact the UK Security Vetting (UKSV) Helpdesk at uksv-helpdesk@cabinetoffice.gov.uk.
